# Features

* **Syringe Mounted Linear Actuator** The syringe pump works by using a stepper motor to rotate a lead screw. The rotation of this screw causes a carriage to move either forward or backward along the linear actuator. The movement of this carriage moves the pump of a syringe to extrude liquid. The syringe pump is compatible with both 10 mL and 20 mL syringes.
* **1.0 mL/min Volumetric Flow Rate** The syringe pump is pre-programmed to create a constant volumetric flow of 1.0 mL/min after the start button is pressed. It also contains a switch to automatically shut off the pump when the syringe is empty.
* **Forward and Backward Carriage Control** The syringe pump has forward and backward buttons that move the carriage more quickly than the automated flow rate to allow for easy syringe positioning.
* **LED State Indicator** The syringe pump has an LED indicator light that displays what state the pump is currently in. Green indicates that the syringe pump is actively running, yellow indicates that the pump is paused, and red indicates that the loaded syringe is empty.

# Instructions for Use

1. Plug the system into a wall outlet. This powers the external power supply, which in turn powers the Arduino and the rest of the system. The pump turns on automatically once connected.
2. Insert the syringe into the holder by sliding it into the appropriate slot on the end support. Ensure that the flange of the syringe is fully inserted in the slot and that the plunger thumb rest is aligned within the carriage slot.
